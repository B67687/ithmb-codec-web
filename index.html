What it does

Drop a file. Get the image.

Each file is decoded on your device and ready to save. One image per file.

The formats old iPods used

RGB555, RGB565, YCbCr 4:2:0, UYVY 4:2:2 and the other formats old iPods used, including embedded JPEG thumbnails.

Four formats, plus ZIP

Save each image as JPEG, PNG, BMP or WebP, or grab the whole batch as a ZIP archive.

Runs in your browser

Chrome, Firefox, Safari or Edge on Windows, macOS, Linux or a phone. Nothing to install.

Local by default.

The decoder runs in your browser. Nothing leaves your device.

  • Files are decoded in the browser
  • Nothing is sent anywhere
  • No account, no install
