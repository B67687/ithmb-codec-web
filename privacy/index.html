Privacy & Terms

Last updated: August 9, 2026

Your files stay on your device

The ITHMB Codec decoder runs entirely in your browser. Decoding is done locally by the WebAssembly build of the open-source Ithmb-Codec library — your .ithmb and .ipm files never leave your device, and nothing is uploaded by simply using the decoder.

Telemetry — strictly opt-in

Nothing is sent anywhere unless you click a Share or Report button on a decode card. Those actions help improve decoder support for real-world files. What each action sends:

  • Share 16 bytes — the file's format prefix (first 16 bytes) plus its size and decode status.
  • Share full file — the same metadata plus the file itself, so an unknown format can be analyzed.
  • Report an issue — the metadata plus your chosen issue category and any detail you type.

Submissions are rate-limited, and IP-derived identifiers are pseudonymized with a keyed hash — your raw IP address is never stored.

Full files you choose to share are stored quarantined, opened only in an offline sandbox on the maintainer machine, scanned locally, and never sent to third-party scanners without your explicit per-file consent. Stored telemetry auto-expires from KV after 365 days, and the operator deletes shared files within 90 days of triage.

What we don't do

  • No cookies.
  • No third-party trackers or analytics.
  • No advertising.
  • No account, no login, no personal data collected from you.

The only thing stored on your device is your language preference (localStorage), which never leaves your browser.

Open source

The decoder, the underlying codec, and this website are MIT-licensed and open source. You can read exactly what the telemetry worker does in the web repository.
